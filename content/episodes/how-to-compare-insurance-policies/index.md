The final episode in the **Insurance Made Simple** series explains how to compare policies based on the protection they provide, not just the price. It covers how to spot unnecessary coverage and gaps that could leave you underinsured.

**In this episode**

- Compare premiums, deductibles, limits, exclusions, and policy terms
- Understand how out-of-pocket costs affect a policy's overall value
- Avoid common mistakes when choosing auto, health, renters, homeowners, life, or disability insurance
- Revisit your coverage as your financial situation changes
